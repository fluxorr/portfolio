# Memoir asset credits

- The drafting proofs, archive tray, and reading loupe are original isometric illustrations. Their outlines were projected and constructed with the [Hairline](https://lucasmarkes.com/lab/hairline) geometry engine, then exported as site-native SVGs.
- The folded-line Memoir mark, SVG threads, procedural halftone trace, sample record diagrams, and 1200 × 630 social cover were designed for this showcase. The sample interface depicts fictional notes and decisions.
- The workspace framing adapts StageShow’s approach to framing a product. [StageShow license](LICENSE-STAGESHOW.txt)
- MindCloud was a reference for page pacing and product storytelling. All artwork and product UI in Memoir are original.
