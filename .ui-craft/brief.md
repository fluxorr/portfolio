# byflux

## Purpose

Give founders a direct way to see Rahul Chaudhari’s website work and start a design or development project.

## Principles

- Use Mobbin’s restraint in the navigation and type, DOVV Studio’s direct service positioning, and Cofounder’s commitment to an art-directed first impression.
- Use black typography and white space to give the project screenshots room to read.
- Build the hero gallery from real project screenshots. Visitors can open each featured website directly from its preview.
- Make motion brief and purposeful, with a complete static experience for reduced motion.

## Learned constraints

- The portfolio needs contemporary, crisp visual art. Landscapes, pixel art, and pastoral illustration are outside the direction.
- Keep Hairline, staged product demos, and the previous portfolio projects out of the design.
