# Atelier / 05

Atelier lives inside the portfolio’s Components section. Each study opens on its own route with a full working tool. The Slips wallet and earlier experiments remain available from the showcase.

| Study | Layouts considered | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Palimpsest | Centered exhibit with records underneath, or offset cabinet with a narrow catalog column | Offset cabinet. Empty blue space on the left balances the dense engraving and gives the evidence labels a fixed place. |
| Isobar | Familiar row of forecast tiles, or a typographic broadside with one 48-hour field | Broadside. Large readings anchor the page while the field makes the shape of the forecast visible. |
| Switchyard | A circular dial, or an in-line circuit with explicit branches | Circuit. A single strategy axis leaves room to show which gates the policy actually bypasses. |
| Figure / Zero | Floating tools around the symbol, or a drafting table with two restrained side rails | Drafting table. The mark owns the center and the precision inputs stay in predictable positions. |
| Meridian | Stacked clock faces, or woven rows on one UTC ruler | Woven rows. A common reference makes daylight-saving changes, midnight, and genuine overlap visible. |

Geist handles interface copy, Instrument Serif is reserved for expressive display, and Geist Mono supports measurements. Each tool owns its own palette. The gallery uses bone, ink, and a restrained red index.

The visual references informed density, construction detail, and the pacing of state changes. All five visuals are built for these instruments. The Asterion Register and Tidal Index are original vector artwork.

## Second pass

The original oversized serif opener and alternating preview rows repeated the same visual decision on every piece. The revised index takes its cues from the artwork-first framing on [Recent](https://recent.design/), the readable interfaces on [Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/), and the single, recognizable visual premise on [Cofounder](https://cofounder.co/). It starts with the full Asterion engraving, places the forecast broadside beside the hardware circuit at unequal sizes, and gives the drafting plate and time loom their own wide spreads. The study pages use compact headers so the working tools come into view sooner.

| Study | What the review exposed | Correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Palimpsest | A large outer heading and a four-line inner title competed with the engraving. | Reduced the outer masthead and tightened the inner title into three deliberate lines. The art takes the wider part of the cabinet and evidence selection lights a distinct section of the paper trail. |
| Isobar | The first preview squashed the glyph field into the center while the axis still ran full width. | Let the plot fill the printed panel and kept the unit, timestamps, and selected band on the same scale. |
| Switchyard | The routing diagram made the nominal path clearer than the allocated effort. | Added physical contact strips whose lit segments encode the actual stage weights. |
| Figure / Zero | The gallery spread made three different construction systems look like one. | Added Fold and Bloom proofs beside Orbit and gave the editor a marked drafting scale. |
| Meridian | On a phone the default selected aperture was outside the visible part of the ruler. | Kept the city labels in view while the ruler follows the selected appointment into view. |
